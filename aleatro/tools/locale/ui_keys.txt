# --- how to play (one paragraph per key)
Select up to 5 cards and play a poker hand. Each hand type has base Chips and Mult; scored cards add their rank in Chips (faces 10, Aces 11).
Score = Chips x Mult. Reach the blind's target before you run out of hands. Discards let you swap cards.
Each ante has a Small Blind, a Big Blind and a Boss Blind with a nasty twist. Beat ante 8 to win.
Spend money in the shop on Charms (passive bonuses, up to 5) and Runes (level up a hand type). You earn $1 interest per $5 held, up to $5.
Some charms and vouchers start locked: the Collection shows what unlocks each one.
Drag cards or charms to reorder them; hold a card to see its details. Gamepad: d-pad moves, A selects (hold A + left/right to move a card), X plays, Y discards, LB/RB sort, Start opens the menu. Keyboard: arrows, Enter, P, D, Q/E, Esc; hold R to restart.
