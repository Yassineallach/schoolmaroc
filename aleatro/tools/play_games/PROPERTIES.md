# Play Games Services properties

Go to **Play Console > Aleatro > Play Games Services > Setup and management >
Configuration > Properties** (Edit properties).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Display name | **Aleatro** (the same in every language) |
| Category | **Card** |
| Icon | [`properties/icon_512.png`](properties/icon_512.png): 512×512 PNG, square (Play rounds the corners itself) |
| Feature graphic | [`properties/feature_1024x500.png`](properties/feature_1024x500.png): 1024×500 PNG, no transparency |
| Default language | English (United States) `en-US` |

Then **Add translations** for the 9 languages below. These are the languages
the achievement import needs (otherwise it fails with "Locale not supported").
For each, enter the display name **Aleatro** and the description.

## Description

### English (United States) `en-US`, the default

Aleatro is a poker roguelike. Play poker hands to score Chips times Mult and beat the target. Each ante brings a Small Blind, a Big Blind and a Boss Blind with a nasty twist. Spend your winnings on charms, spells, runes and vouchers that bend the rules, and build a combo strong enough to beat ante 8.

150 charms, 28 boss blinds, 15 decks, 8 stakes and 30 challenges. Play offline, in 10 languages, with touch or a gamepad.

## Regenerating the art

- Icon: the app icon (`res://icon.png`) over its own background
  (`res://icon_background.png`), so the corners are filled.
- Feature graphic: `xvfb-run godot --rendering-driver opengl3 --resolution 1024x500 -s tools/play_games/feature_graphic.gd`
  (from the project folder). It draws the title's swirl, logo, cards and charms
  with the game's own code.
