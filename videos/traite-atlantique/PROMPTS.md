# Image prompts: La traite atlantique (ChatGPT, one ink-sketch hand)

This is an infinite-canvas film. Every drawing is one subject on plain white. In the video the white
becomes transparent and the pen redraws the lines on the parchment board (lines only, no fill).

**Reference: attach the approved `G01.png` to every prompt below**, so the whole film shares one
hand. G01 (the dark-skinned bound hands) is approved and in `assets/`.

Rules:
1. Generate a few at a time (one act is a good batch) and send them here for checking.
2. Fix with short follow-ups, one change per message (« Same drawing, rope of twisted plant fibre, not modern cord »).
3. Black ink only. No colour, grey wash, gradients, 3D or anime, and no text or letters.
4. One subject per drawing, on plain white.
5. **Historical accuracy:** every drawing shows something documented for the 1500s–1800s (period
   clothing, ships, objects). No modern items: no metal zips, modern rope, rifles before their time.
6. **Skin and dignity:** African people are drawn with dark skin in dense cross-hatching, as in G01. Europeans are left lighter.
   **Dignity:** African people are drawn as individuals, with real faces, as carefully as anyone
   else. No caricature, no exaggerated features. No real person's likeness.
7. **Violence implied:** never a wound, blood or the moment of a blow.
8. Name each file by its code (`G01.png`, `G02.png`…) and upload them here (a zip is fine).

Maps (M01–M06) are drawn in code: they need no prompts.
