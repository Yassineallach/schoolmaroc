# L’histoire du sel — storyboard (draft for approval)

Narration: 13 clips, about 4 min 05 s. History identity (parchment, oxblood, sepia prints), astrolabe background.

**Text rule for this film (user's request): as few kinetic-text moments as possible.**
Images carry the narration. No word-by-word headlines, no labels that repeat the voice,
no chapter kickers. Static photo captions (small, bottom-right, describing what the image is)
are kept, because they are not animated text. Animated type is used **only** where the voice alone
cannot make the idea land:

| # | Where | Why it is needed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | clip 3, « chlorure de sodium » | `NaCl`, written once: the formula is the image of the word |
| 2 | clip 9, « salaire … sal » | SALARIUM with SAL marked: an etymology has to be seen |
| 3 | clip 10, « au-dessus du sel / en dessous » | a single drawn line across the banquet table, no words |

That is two typed moments in the whole film (plus one drawn line). Everything else is pictures.

## Ending (the user's note): the whole board

The last scene wraps everything up: after the last drawing, the camera zooms out slowly until the
whole board is in view, every drawing and handwritten word made during the film, laid out on the
one parchment, and holds there for the end. (To build in act 5 with board.py: a final camera stop
that frames the bounding box of every placed drawing.)
