# L'énigme des chapeaux des prisonniers : drawings (ChatGPT)

Style for every drawing: a 19th-century French wood engraving like the Hetzel editions of Jules Verne.
Black ink lines and cross-hatching on white paper, with no colour and no grey wash.

Style reference: `style_reference.png`, cropped from *20 000 lieues sous les mers* (Hetzel, 1871):
the Nautilus engine room, public domain (Wikimedia Commons, « 20000 Nautilus engines.jpg »).
Once G03 is approved, it replaces this reference for every later drawing.

The film prints each drawing in its own ink on its paper (`cutout.ink_image`), captioned « Illustration ».
Save each drawing as `assets/G0x.png`. Until a file exists, the act shows a labelled placeholder.

## G03 · the ten prisoners in profile (first, on its own)

Carries acts 1, 3 and 4. The code drops paper hats onto the heads, so the heads must stay bare.

```
Use the attached engraving only for its style: 19th-century French wood engraving, black ink lines and cross-hatching on white paper, no colour, no grey wash. Ignore its subject (the machines, the pipes, the room).

Draw ten adults standing in one single line, seen exactly from the side, all facing right, one behind the other, with a small gap between them so that nobody overlaps. They are sorted by height: the tallest on the far left, the shortest on the far right, the heights going down steadily. Men and women mixed, plain everyday clothes of the 1880s, arms at their sides, calm faces, all bareheaded (no hats, no caps, no hair ornaments). Full figures from head to feet, feet on one shared ground line. Plain white background: no floor, no shadows, no scenery. Wide 16:9 image, the line filling the width.
```
