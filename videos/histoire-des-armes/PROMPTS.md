# Image prompts — Histoire des armes (ChatGPT, ink engraving style)

Style: black pen-and-ink engravings like your reference (image 1). Every drawing is cut out
in code: the white paper turns transparent and the lines become the film's walnut ink, so
each drawing looks printed on the astrolabe parchment.

**Before you start:** crop your reference to ONE clean drawing, the helmet works best,
with no watermark and as little grid as possible. Attach it to every prompt.

How to use:

1. Generate **P02 first, alone**. When you like it, attach P02 (instead of the helmet) to
   every later prompt.
2. Fix with short follow-ups, one change per message ("Same image, less cross-hatching").
3. No colour, no grey wash, no anime, no 3D. Real people are never drawn.
4. Name each file by its code (`P02.png`, `C04.png`, …) and upload them here.
