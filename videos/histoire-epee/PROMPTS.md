# Image prompts — L’histoire de l’épée (ChatGPT, one ink-sketch hand)

Infinite-canvas film: every drawing is one subject on plain white. In the video the white becomes
transparent and the pen redraws the lines on the parchment board (lines only, no fill).

**Reference:** attach `style_reference.png` (the ink goat from *L’histoire du sel*) to **G01**.
Once G01 is approved, attach **G01** instead to every later prompt, so the whole film shares one hand.

Rules:
1. Generate **G01 first, alone**, and send it here for checking.
2. Fix with short follow-ups, one change per message (« Same drawing, no plumes on the helmets »).
3. Black ink only. No colour, grey wash, gradients, 3D or anime, and no text or letters.
4. One subject per drawing, on plain white.
5. No real person’s face: Philippe V is seen from behind, Ali ibn Abi Talib never appears, Tite-Live is an anonymous scribe.
6. Name each file by its code (`G01.png`, `G02.png`…) and upload them here (a zip is fine).
7. Check period details: no stirrups on Romans, Macedonian helmets not Spartan, no modern items before act 4.

Maps (M01–M04) and the dashed sword outline (C01) are drawn in code: no prompts.
