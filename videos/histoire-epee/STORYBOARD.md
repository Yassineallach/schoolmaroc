# L’histoire de l’épée — storyboard (draft for approval)

Narration: 14 clips, about 4 min 41 s after the pauses were lengthened (3.4 s of silence added at
sentence ends). Style: **infinite canvas**. One parchment board, a single pen draws one ChatGPT ink
sketch per view and writes the few words by hand. There are no cuts. History identity (parchment,
oxblood ink), with an original music bed composed in code.

**Text: minimal.** The pen writes only places, dates, a term or two, and numbers the voice states.
No word on screen repeats a sentence. Everything written is listed in the right-hand column (✎).

**Returning motif: the distance line.** A dashed pen line is drawn between the one who strikes and
the one who is struck. At arm's length with swords (act 4) it is short; it grows longer with the
rifle, the artillery and the drone. With the machine (act 5) there is no person at one end. The film
ends where the human story began: on a **human hand** (G07, then G46).

**Violence is implied only.** For clip 2 (severed arms and heads), the soldiers stand frozen around a
body covered by a cloak. No wound is drawn.

**Real people are never drawn as likenesses.** Philippe V is seen only from behind. Ali ibn Abi Talib
is not depicted at all; only the sword Dhou al-Fiqar is drawn. Tite-Live has no authentic portrait,
so he appears as an anonymous scribe from behind, captioned « Illustration ». Grossman, Guterres and
the CICR are represented by objects (a book, a hall, a treaty), not faces or emblems.
