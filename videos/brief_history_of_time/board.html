Step 1

The brief

Which image tool you use and which style to follow. With a style reference, Claude rewrites the prompts in the short form: the reference image plus one line naming the subject.

Step 2

Acts and shots

Each line of narration with the visual it triggers. Red tags name the asset a shot needs. Everything else is drawn in code.

Step 3

Asset slots

Copy a prompt, generate the image, then drop it in the slot. Plates keep their painted background. Cut-outs go on plain ivory, and Claude checks every edge for leftovers before using one. Real people come from photos only.

Step 4

Act reviews

Claude posts a contact sheet and a draft render for each act. Approve it, or say what to change.
