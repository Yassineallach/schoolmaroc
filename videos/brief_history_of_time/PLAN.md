# A Brief History of Time: explainer plan

15 narration clips, 4:15 total. Same look as Red Rising: grey paper, cut-outs with a red marker stroke, torn photo prints, documents, stamps and typed captions drawn in code. Every reveal lands on the spoken word.

## Acts and shot list

| Act | Title | Clips | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Silent Manuscript | 1–4 | 47 s |
| 2 | A Book Without Equations | 5–7 | 64 s |
| 3 | The Edge of a Black Hole | 8–10 | 54 s |
| 4 | The Arrow of Time | 11–12 | 45 s |
| 5 | The Most Unfinished Bestseller | 13–15 | 45 s |

### Act 1: The Silent Manuscript (clips 1–4)
- "In 1985… Swiss hospital… ventilator": **E01** hospital plate, slow push-in. Typed caption `SWITZERLAND, 1985`.
- "Pneumonia… wasting disease": hospital chart document (code): `Pneumonia`, `Motor neurone disease`, with a marker circle.
- "switching the machine off": a big `OFF?` switch drawn in code, red marker circle.
- "His wife refuses": stamp `REFUSED`.
- "opening in his windpipe… survives": chart line `Tracheotomy`, ticked.
- "takes what little remained of his voice": a speech-bubble cut-out drawn in code, struck through.
- "Back in England… unfinished manuscript": **E02** desk-with-manuscript plate, with a typed page sheet slid on top.
- "The man is Stephen Hawking": **R1** portrait (your photo) as a torn print, name headline.
- "A Brief History of Time": book cover drawn in code (our own design, not the real cover art).
- "April Fool's Day, 1988": calendar sheet `1 APRIL 1988`.
- "birth, structure, and possible fate": three typed labels with marker arrows.

### Act 2: A Book Without Equations (clips 5–7)
- "almost no mathematics": a textbook page full of equations, struck through.
- "every equation would cut his sales in half": a falling bar chart (½, ¼, ⅛…).
- "kept exactly one: E = mc²": big headline `E = mc²`, red circle.
- "diagrams, thought experiments, stories": three cards.
- "from Aristotle to Galileo to Einstein": a timeline of three name cards. If you send public-domain portraits (R2–R4), they go on the cards. Without them, the cards are typographic.
- "1920s… galaxies rushing away": **E03** observatory plate, then an expansion diagram drawn in code (galaxies moving apart, with arrows).
- "Run that expansion backward… the Big Bang": the dots converge to one point, then flash, with a `THE BIG BANG` headline.
- "physics breaks… two great theories": two documents, `GENERAL RELATIVITY` and `QUANTUM MECHANICS`, drawn in code.
- "bending of space and time": a warped grid with a planet.
- "certainty dissolves into probability": a probability cloud of dots.
- "Squeeze the universe small enough": the two sheets collide, with a `CONFLICT` stamp.

### Act 3: The Edge of a Black Hole (clips 8–10)
- "massive star burns out… collapse": **E04** deep-space plate, then a collapsing-star diagram drawn in code.
- "not even light… event horizon": a black disc, a labelled ring and light-ray arrows that bend in.
- "black holes are not entirely black… faint glow… evaporate": a red glow ring whose disc shrinks over a `× billions of years` caption.
- "1963, at the age of 21… two years to live": a diagnosis card document.
- "solve problems as shapes in his head": geometric shapes floating up.
- "raising his eyebrows at letters on a card": a letter board drawn in code that spells a word letter by letter.
- "computer program… synthesizer": a screen drawn in code with a word list, plus waveform bars. **P03** wheelchair cut-out.

### Act 4: The Arrow of Time (clips 11–12)
- "hidden in its title": the book title, with `TIME` circled.
- "It has a direction": a big red arrow.
- "A teacup slides off a table and shatters": **P01** cup on the table edge, then **P02** shards (cut-outs), with shake.
- "shards leaping up and fusing back": the shards play in reverse, ghosted, with a `NEVER SEEN` stamp.
- "disorder, memory, expansion": three labelled arrows all pointing the same way.
- "north of the North Pole": a globe drawn in code with converging meridians and an arrow going "north" off the top that meets nothing.
- "finite and still have no edge": a closed sphere outline, captioned `NO EDGE`.

### Act 5: The Most Unfinished Bestseller (clips 13–15)
- "A Short History of Time… changed one word": the title typed, `Short` struck through, `Brief` written in marker.
- "237 weeks… Sunday Times": a bestseller-list document with a counter that ticks to 237.
- "more than 10 million copies": a stack of book spines, and `10,000,000+`.
- "most bought and least finished": a bookmark stuck at an early page, with a stamp `UNFINISHED`.
- "complete theory… ordinary people join the debate": **E05** night sky with small figures.
- "know the mind of God": the quote, typed.
- "lost his voice… oldest question… night sky": **E05** held, end title.

## Assets I need from you

These look bad when drawn in code: people, places, organic objects. Everything else (documents, diagrams, the book cover, the globe, the letter board, charts) I draw.

**Real people. Photos only, never generated:**
- **R1**: a photo of Stephen Hawking that you have the rights to use (portrait, ideally around the 1980s).
- *(optional)* **R2–R4**: public-domain portraits of Aristotle (a bust), Galileo and Einstein. Without them, the timeline cards are typographic.

**To generate.** These are default storybook-style prompts. Tell me your tool and style first, and I'll rewrite them in the short reference-image form if you have a style reference.

Plates (16:9, painted background, used as-is):

- **E01**: Hospital room at night in 1985, a patient lying in bed seen from the foot of the bed, face hidden in shadow, a bulky 1980s ventilator with tubes and a monitor beside the bed, cold blue night light from a window, warm small lamp.
- **E02**: A cluttered English academic study on a grey rainy day. A wooden desk with a thick stack of handwritten manuscript pages, a fountain pen, a chalkboard with faint diagrams behind it, an empty chair.
- **E03**: A 1920s mountain observatory at night, the dome open, a large brass telescope pointing at a star-filled sky, a tiny astronomer silhouette at the eyepiece.
- **E04**: Deep space with a few distant spiral galaxies and one huge glowing star, painted storybook style, dark indigo and muted gold.
- **E05**: A hillside at night under a vast starry sky with the Milky Way. Small silhouettes of a few ordinary people of different ages looking up, seen from behind.

Cut-outs (one object, plain warm ivory background):

- **P01**: A white porcelain teacup with saucer, half full of tea, tipping off the edge of a small wooden table. Show the table edge too.
- **P02**: The same teacup shattered into about 8 separate pieces, scattered flat with a small tea splash, seen from slightly above.
- **P03**: An empty 1980s motorised wheelchair, three-quarter view.

Append this to every prompt above:

```

Organic hand-drawn outlines in deep brown (#3B2A20), medium weight around the silhouette and thinner inside, with slight irregularity. Flat color fills with one shadow tone, one light tone and only a few small highlights; restrained painterly shading; subtle paper grain and light brush texture; muted earthy palette of warm ivory, terracotta, rust red, muted blue, desaturated green, deep brown and muted gold; soft warm directional light. NOT anime, no 3D render, no photorealism, no glossy vector style, no heavy gradients, no glow. No text, no labels.

For the plates, add: `16:9 widescreen.` For the cut-outs, add: `Plain empty warm ivory background, one object only, fully visible.`
