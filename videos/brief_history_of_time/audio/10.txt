He did this work against his own deadline. In 1963, at the age of 21, Hawking was diagnosed with motor neurone disease and given about two years to live. As his hands failed, he learned to solve problems as shapes in his head rather than equations on paper. After the tracheotomy, he spelled out words by raising his eyebrows at letters on a card, until a computer program let him pick words from a screen and speak them through a synthesizer.
